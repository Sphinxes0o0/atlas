# Flexibility & Mobility Training (灵活性与柔韧性训练)

## Overview

Flexibility and mobility training are critical components of athletic preparation and injury prevention. Evidence supports distinct protocols for different goals — dynamic stretching for warm-up, static stretching for chronic flexibility, PNF for rapid gains, and foam rolling for recovery.

## Stretching Methods Compared

| Method | Best Use | Evidence |
|--------|---------|---------|
| Dynamic stretching | Pre-exercise warm-up | Potentiates performance; no strength impairment |
| Static stretching | Post-exercise, dedicated flexibility sessions | Effective for chronic ROM improvement |
| PNF stretching | Rapid flexibility gains | Sit-and-reach +24.3% vs +10.9% (8 weeks) |
| Foam rolling | Pre/post exercise, recovery | ↑ROM without impairing force |

## Key Evidence

### Dynamic vs Static Stretching

- **Static stretching** pre-exercise: may cause small reductions in strength/power (Behara & Singh 2024)
- **Dynamic stretching** pre-exercise: potentiates subsequent performance
- **Optimal sequence**: static → dynamic can nullify negative effects

### Injury Prevention

- Stretching + warm-up programs can prevent muscle injuries (meta-analysis 2024)
- Static stretching alone: inconsistent injury prevention evidence
- Limited ROM is an **independent risk factor** for muscle strains, especially hamstrings

### ROM Enhancement

| Factor | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| Limited ROM | ↑ injury risk (independent factor) |
| Increased ROM | Greater arc before tissue stress reaches injury threshold |
| FMS scores | Targeted intervention: injury rate 15% → 2.5% |

### PNF Stretching

- **Mechanism**: Minimizes stretch reflex via autogenic inhibition
- **Effect**: 24.3% sit-and-reach improvement vs 10.9% (dynamic/static) after 8 weeks
- **Application**: Use when rapid flexibility gains are needed

### Foam Rolling / Self-Myofascial Release

- Increases ROM without compromising force production
- Effective as warm-up preparation and post-exercise recovery
- Mechanism: fascial remodeling + neural desensitization
