# Hip — Gluteal, Iliopsoas & Deep Rotators

## Anatomy Overview

### Gluteus Maximus

**Origin:** Posterior gluteal line of ilium, sacrum, coccyx, sacrotuberous ligament

**Insertion:** Gluteal tuberosity of femur, iliotibial band (upper 1/3)

**Innervation:** Superior gluteal nerve (L5-S2)

Largest muscle in the body by cross-sectional area. Primary hip extensor + external rotator. Essential for running, squatting, climbing. Force couple with iliopsoas for hip extension during running. [[entities/exercise-science/biomechanics/muscle-anatomy/thigh]] (hamstrings are hip extensors but function differently — glute max dominant in power, hamstrings dominant in knee flexion).

### Gluteus Medius

**Origin:** External surface of ilium (between anterior and posterior gluteal lines)

**Insertion:** Greater trochanter of femur

**Innervation:** Superior gluteal nerve (L4-S1)

Primary hip abductor. Trendelenburg's test assesses unilateral gluteus medius function. Primary frontal plane stabilizer during single-leg stance in running/walking. [[entities/exercise-science/biomechanics/muscle-anatomy/lower-leg]] (gluteus medius weakness linked to IT band syndrome via TT band).

### Gluteus Minimus

**Origin:** External ilium between anterior and inferior gluteal lines

**Insertion:** Anterior facet of greater trochanter

**Innervation:** Superior gluteal nerve (L4-S1)

Smallest glute. Anterior fibers — hip internal rotation. Middle/post fibers — abduction. Deep stabilizer; works with medius for femoral head centering.

### Iliopsoas (Psoas Major + Iliacus)

**Origin:** Psoas — vertebral bodies T12-L5, transverse processes L1-L5; Iliacus — iliac fossa

**Insertion:** Lesser trochanter of femur

**Innervation:** Ventral rami L1-L3

Primary hip flexor. Only true hip flexor attaching to the spine (psoas). Tight/short iliopsoas common in sedentary populations and runners. Linked to lumbar spine mechanics — can increase lumbar lordosis. [[entities/exercise-science/biomechanics/muscle-anatomy/core]] (psoas shares thoracolumbar fascia connection; iliopsoas tension affects lumbar posture).

### Tensor Fasciae Latae (TFL)

**Origin:** Anterior superior iliac spine, anterior iliac crest

**Insertion:** IT band (via iliotibial tract)

**Innervation:** Superior gluteal nerve (L4-S1)

Hip flexor + abductor + internal rotator. Shares IT band with gluteus maximus. Overactivity → IT band syndrome and knee valgus. [[entities/exercise-science/biomechanics/muscle-anatomy/thigh]] (TFL-IT band distributes force to knee; vastus lateralis shares common fascia).

### Deep Hip Rotators (External)

**Piriformis:** Origin — anterior sacrum; Insertion — greater trochanter. Primary external rotator of femur; also assists abduction when hip is flexed. Sciatic nerve runs inferior to piriformis (piriformis syndrome).

**Other deep rotators:** Quadratus femoris, gemellus superior/inferior, obturator internus/externus. All originate on pelvis, insert on greater trochanter. Provide fine control of femoral rotation.

### Other Hip Muscles

**Pectineus:** Adductor + hip flexor (C5-L2). Shared innervation with adductors and iliopsoas.

**Adductor Group (Gracilis, Adductor Magnus/Brevis/Longus):** Hip adduction + some flexion (pectineus, adductor longus). Common site of strain in athletes (groin strain). [[entities/exercise-science/biomechanics/muscle-anatomy/thigh]] (adductors are medial thigh muscles).

## Function in Exercise & Running

### Gluteus Maximus Activation

| Exercise | Gluteus Maximus EMG |
|----------|--------------------|
| Hip thrust | 80-100% MVIC |
| Deadlift | 70-90% MVIC |
| Squat | 50-80% MVIC |
| Step-ups | 60-80% MVIC |
| Running (stance phase) | 40-60% MVIC |

### Gluteus Medius Activation

| Exercise | Gluteus Medius EMG |
|----------|-------------------|
| Clamshell | 50-70% MVIC |
| Lateral band walks | 60-80% MVIC |
| Single-leg squat | 50-70% MVIC |
| Trendelenburg hold | 60-80% MVIC |

### Hip in Running

- **Stance phase:** Gluteus maximus extends hip; gluteus medius stabilizes pelvis in frontal plane
- **Swing phase:** Iliopsoas flexes hip; TFL assists with hip flexion + IT band tension
- **Deceleration:** Hip flexors eccentrically control hip extension at end of swing phase
- **Landing:** Gluteus medius prevents Trendelenburg drop of contralateral pelvis

### Force Couples

- **Hip extension:** Gluteus maximus + hamstrings (glute max dominant in powerful extension)
- **Hip flexion:** Iliopsoas + rectus femoris + TFL (iliopsoas dominant)
- **Frontal plane:** Gluteus medius/minimus vs TFL + adductors (gluteus medius dominant)
- **Transverse plane:** External rotators (piriformis, quadratus femoris) vs internal rotators (gluteus minimus, TFL)

## Common Injuries

| Structure | Injury | Mechanism |
|-----------|--------|-----------|
| Gluteus maximus | Strain / tendinopathy | Sprinting; heavy hip extension; downhill running |
| Gluteus medius | Tendinopathy / weakness | Excessive loading; Trendelenburg gait; IT band syndrome |
| Gluteus minimus | Strain / atrophy | Disuse; hip pathology |
| Iliopsoas | Tightness / tendinopathy | Chronic sitting; running; hip impingement |
| TFL | Overactivity / IT band syndrome | Gluteus medius weakness; knee valgus; running |
| Piriformis | Syndrome (sciatica mimic) | External rotator overuse; sitting; hip dysfunction |
| Adductors | Groin strain | Rapid change of direction; hip abduction against resistance |

**Piriformis syndrome** — sciatic nerve entrapment under piriformis. Pain in posterior hip and radiating down leg. Often misdiagnosed as sciatica.

**Greater trochanteric pain syndrome (GTPS)** — previously called trochanteric bursitis. Gluteus medius/minimus tendinopathy + IT band involvement.

**Hip impingement (FAI)** — cam or pincer morphology. Labral tears. Anterior hip/groin pain with flexion activities.

**Gluteal amnesia** — inhibition of gluteus maximus activation post-inactivity or low back pain. Brain fails to recruit glute max during hip extension.

## Training Recommendations

### Gluteus Maximus Strengthening

**Compound movements first:**
- Hip thrust: 3-5 sets × 5-8 reps (strength)
- Deadlift: 3-5 sets × 5-8 reps (hip hinge)
- Squat: 3-5 sets × 8-12 reps (hypertrophy)

**Isolation exercises:**
- Glute bridge: 3×15-20 (activation)
- Cable pull-through: 3×12-15 (glute emphasis)
- Reverse hyperextension: 3×12-15

### Gluteus Medius Strengthening

**Frontal plane focus:**
- Clamshells: 3×15-20 per side
- Lateral band walks: 3×20 steps each direction
- Single-leg RDL: 3×10 per side
- Side-lying hip abduction: 3×15 per side

### Hip Flexor Management

- **Stretch:** Half-kneeling hip flexor stretch; hold 30-60s
- **Strengthen eccentrically:** Nordic hip extension; resisted hip flexion
- **Postural correction:** Reduce time in shortened position (sitting)
- **IT band / TFL:** Address overactive TFL with foam rolling + gluteus medius strengthening

### Deep Hip Rotator Training

- **Cable external rotation:** 3×12-15 per side
- **Prone hip ER with band:** 3×12-15
- **Clamshell with ER:** Emphasizes external rotation component
