# Creatine (肌酸)

## Overview

Creatine is one of the most researched and effective sports nutrition supplements. As an endogenous amino acid derivative, creatine plays a critical role in ATP regeneration during high-intensity exercise through the ATP-PCr (phosphocreatine) system.

## Biochemistry

### Creatine-Phosphocreatine System

```
Phosphocreatine + ADP ⇌ Creatine + ATP

PCr energy bond: 10,300 cal/mol (vs ATP: 7,300 cal/mol)
Muscle PCr stores: 2-4× ATP levels
Reaction time: milliseconds (immediate energy)
```

| Exercise Duration | Primary Energy System |
|------------------|----------------------|
| 0-6 seconds | ATP-PCr (creatine-dependent) |
| 6 seconds - 2 minutes | ATP-PCr + glycolysis |
| >2 minutes | Aerobic oxidation |

## Effects

### Strength & Power (Strong Evidence)

- Muscle creatine content: **+15-30%**
- Phosphocreatine reserves: **+15-40%**
- Power output (1-2s max effort): **↑ significant**
- Repeated sprint ability: **+10-15%**

ISSN Position (2017): Creatine monohydrate is the **only** form with substantial evidence for bioavailability, efficacy, and safety.

### Muscle Hypertrophy

Creatine promotes growth through:
1. Increased training volume (more work capacity)
2. Cell hydration (osmotic effect → cell swelling)
3. IGF-1 expression upregulation
4. Better ATP availability for protein synthesis

### Endurance Performance

**No significant benefit** for pure endurance athletes.

## Dosing Protocols

| Phase | Dose | Duration | Purpose |
|-------|------|----------|---------|
| Loading | 0.3 g/kg/day | 5-7 days | Rapid muscle saturation |
| Maintenance | 3-5 g/day | Ongoing | Maintain saturation |
| Simplified | 3-5 g/day | 3-4 weeks | No loading, slower saturation |

**With carbs**: Muscle PCr stores +38% vs creatine alone (insulin促进CreaT uptake)

## Safety

| Concern | Evidence |
|---------|---------|
| Kidney damage | **No evidence** — normal kidney function maintained |
| Dehydration/cramps | **No causal link** — still hydrate properly |
| Hair loss | **No scientific evidence** |
| It is not a steroid | Correct — endogenous compound, not hormonal |

Long-term (>5 year) studies show **no adverse effects**.

## Special Populations

### Sarcopenia Prevention (Elderly)

Meta-analysis (Chilibeck 2017): Creatine + resistance training significantly increases lean body mass, bench press strength, and leg press strength in older adults.

### Vegetarian Athletes

**Greater benefit** for vegetarians — meat is the primary dietary creatine source; vegetarians start with lower baseline stores.

## Limitations

- Not effective for pure endurance performance
- Water retention: +0.8-1.5 kg weight gain (mostly water)
- GI upset possible with large doses or empty stomach
- Not recommended for children/adolescents (limited data)
