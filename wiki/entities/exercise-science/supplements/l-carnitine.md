# L-Carnitine (左旋肉碱)

## Overview

L-Carnitine (beta-hydroxy-gamma-N-trimethylaminobutyric acid) is a conditionally essential nutrient synthesized from lysine and methionine in the liver, kidneys, and brain. Its fundamental role is facilitating fatty acid transport into mitochondria for beta-oxidation.

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| Daily synthesis | 11-34 mg (healthy adults) |
| Primary dietary source | Red meat (85-180 mg/100g) |
| Plasma concentration | 40-60 μmol/L |
| Bioavailability | Limited in non-vegetarians; higher in vegetarians |

## Mechanism of Action

### Carnitine Shuttle System (CPT System)

```
Long-chain fatty acyl-CoA
        ↓ CPT-I (outer mitochondrial membrane)
Acylcarnitine (via CACT translocase)
        ↓ CPT-II (inner mitochondrial membrane)
Fatty acyl-CoA → β-oxidation → ATP
```

### Additional Functions

- **Acetyl buffer**: Forms acetylcarnitine, maintaining CoA/acetyl-CoA ratio
- **Propionylcarnitine**: Supports gluconeogenesis (branched-chain amino acid catabolism)
- **Membrane stability**: Antioxidant properties

## Evidence Summary

### Cardiovascular (Strong Evidence)

| Study | Population | Finding |
|-------|-----------|---------|
| Cruz et al. 2018 | Heart failure + CABG (n=28) | 50 mg/kg/day → rapid plasma carnitine recovery; effect size d=0.89-1.34 |

### Psychiatric/Neurological (Promising)

| Study | Population | Finding |
|-------|-----------|---------|
| Nie et al. 2021 | MDD patients (n=89) | Serum ALC ↓ in MDD; AUC=0.898 for diagnosis; treatment responders showed normalized ALC |

### Athletic Performance (Mixed)

| Study | Population | Finding |
|-------|-----------|---------|
| Soop et al. 1988 | Elite walkers (n=6) | 4g/day × 14d → VO2max ↑6% (p<0.02) |
| Greig et al. 1987 | Untrained (n=22) | 2g/day × 2-4 weeks → no VO2max improvement |

### Antioxidant/Lipid (Moderate)

| Study | Population | Finding |
|-------|-----------|---------|
| Hassan et al. 2018 | Pemphigus vulgaris (n=52) | 2g/day × 8 weeks → TOS↓30%, LDL↓19%, TG↓20% |

## Dosing

| Application | Dose | Duration |
|-------------|------|----------|
| Athletic (endurance) | 2-4 g/day | 2-4 weeks minimum |
| Cardiovascular recovery | 50 mg/kg/day | 60 days |
| Antioxidant/lipid | 2 g/day | 8+ weeks |

## TMAO Controversy

Recent research suggests gut microbiome converts L-carnitine → TMA → TMAO (trimethylamine N-oxide), which is associated with cardiovascular risk. However, this relationship is **controversial** — the clinical significance is debated, and the benefits in specific populations (carnitine-deficient, cardiovascular recovery) may outweigh theoretical risks.

## Limitations

- Oral bioavailability limited in non-vegetarians (already consuming carnitine from meat)
- Athletic benefits primarily in trained populations
- TMAO cardiovascular risk hypothesis requires further validation
- Most compelling evidence in clinical populations, not healthy athletes
