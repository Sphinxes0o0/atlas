# Beta-Alanine (β-丙氨酸)

## Overview

Beta-alanine is a non-essential amino acid that acts as the rate-limiting precursor for carnosine synthesis in skeletal muscle. Carnosine (β-alanyl-histidine) serves as an intracellular pH buffer, helping to neutralize hydrogen ions (H⁺) produced during high-intensity exercise, thereby delaying muscular fatigue and extending time to exhaustion.

## Mechanism of Action

### Carnosine Buffering System

During high-intensity exercise (>60% VO2max), anaerobic glycolysis produces lactate + H⁺, causing intracellular acidosis that impairs muscle contraction. Carnosine (pKa = 6.83) buffers the H⁺ accumulated in working muscle, protecting contractile function.

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Muscle carnosine increase | +40-80% after 4-6 weeks |
| Time to exhaustion (TEE) | +13-14% improvement |
| Optimal dose | 3.2-6.4 g/day |
| Time to carnosine saturation | 4-6 weeks |

### Key Studies

- **Trexeth et al. (2012)**: Paresthesia dose-dependence — 800mg threshold identified
- **Decaio et al. (2011)**: Sustained-release formulations reduce paresthesia 48% vs immediate-release
- **Hoffman et al. (2018)**: 4.5g/day × 5 weeks — no serious adverse events

## Dosing Protocol

| Phase | Dose | Duration | Notes |
|-------|------|----------|-------|
| Loading | 3.2-6.4 g/day | 4-6 weeks | Divided into 400-800mg doses every 3-4h |
| Maintenance | 0.8-1.6 g/day | Ongoing | To sustain elevated carnosine |

**Paresthesia management**: Single doses >800mg cause tingling. Use sustained-release or split doses.

## Benefits

- **High-intensity exercise**: TEE improved 13-14%
- **Repeated sprint ability**: +40-80% muscle carnosine
- **4-6 weeks** to achieve full carnosine saturation
- Safety profile excellent up to 24 weeks

## Safety & Side Effects

| Side Effect | Mechanism | Management |
|-------------|-----------|------------|
| Paresthesia (tingling) | TRPV1/TRPA1 receptor activation | Dose <800mg/single dose; sustained-release |
| None serious | — | Up to 24 weeks, no organ toxicity |

**Long-term (>24 weeks)**: Data limited, use with caution.

## Limitations

- Paresthesia uncomfortable but harmless
- Long loading period (4-6 weeks)
- Limited benefit for endurance <60% VO2max
- Long-term safety beyond 24 weeks unknown
