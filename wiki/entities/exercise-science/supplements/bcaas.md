# BCAAs (支链氨基酸)

## Overview

Branched-Chain Amino Acids (BCAAs) comprise three essential amino acids: **leucine**, **isoleucine**, and **valine**. They are unique among amino acids because they are metabolized almost exclusively in skeletal muscle rather than the liver, making them particularly relevant to exercise physiology.

| Ratio | Leucine | Isoleucine | Valine |
|-------|---------|------------|--------|
| Standard 2:1:1 | 50% | 25% | 25% |
| Typical dose | 2.5-5g | 1.25-2.5g | 1.25-2.5g |

## Key Mechanisms

### 1. mTOR Activation (Leucine)

Leucine is the primary BCAA driving muscle protein synthesis via mTORC1 pathway:

| Leucine Dose | Effect |
|-------------|--------|
| ~2.5g/meal | Threshold for maximal MPS stimulation |
| 5g + 30g whey protein | +47% MPS vs protein alone (Morton 2024) |
| >3g | Ceiling effect — no additional benefit |

### 2. CNS Fatigue Reduction (Tryptophan Competition)

During exercise, tryptophan (precursor to 5-HT/serotonin) and BCAAs compete for transport across the blood-brain barrier via the same transporter. Increasing BCAA concentration may reduce CNS-derived fatigue.

### 3. DOMS Reduction

BCAAs reduce delayed onset muscle soreness through:
- Reduced inflammatory cytokine release
- Limited neutrophil infiltration
- Improved muscle recovery markers

## Evidence Summary

| Application | Evidence | Finding |
|------------|----------|---------|
| MPS stimulation | Strong | Leucine threshold ~2.5g activates mTOR |
| DOMS reduction | Moderate | Soreness ↓34% at 72h post-eccentric (Hobson 2024) |
| Glycogen sparing | Moderate | RER ↓0.05; CHO oxidation ↓12% (Zhang 2025) |
| Performance (strength/endurance) | Limited | No consistent ergogenic effect |

## Dosing

| Goal | Dose | Timing |
|------|------|--------|
| Anabolic (MPS) | 5g leucine (or 10-12g total BCAAs) | Post-workout |
| DOMS/recovery | 3.5g Leu / 1.75g Ile / 1.75g Val | Pre/post workout |
| During exercise (fatigue) | 5-10g | During prolonged exercise |

## Critical Limitation

**Complete protein/EAA is superior**: BCAAs alone lack the full amino acid profile needed for sustained muscle protein synthesis. Whole protein or EAA formulations are superior to isolated BCAAs.

The 2026-05 research summary notes: "运动后EAA或蛋白质优于单独BCAAs" — post-exercise EAA or protein outperforms BCAA alone.

## Comparison with Whole Protein

| Factor | BCAAs alone | Whole protein/EAA |
|--------|-------------|-------------------|
| MPS activation | Yes (via leucine) | Yes |
| Full substrate for synthesis | No | Yes |
| Tryptophan competition | Yes | No |
| Cost | Higher per gram | Lower per gram |
| Evidence quality | Moderate | Strong |
