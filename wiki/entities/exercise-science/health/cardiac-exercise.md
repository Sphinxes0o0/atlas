# Cardiac Exercise Benefits (运动与心血管健康)

## Overview

Regular exercise provides profound cardioprotective effects. Cardiorespiratory fitness (VO2max) is the **strongest predictor of survival** — stronger than traditional risk factors like smoking, diabetes, or coronary artery disease.

## VO2max and Mortality

| Finding | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Elite CRF vs sedentary | ~1/5th the death risk |
| Hazard ratio (lowest vs highest fitness) | ~5.04 |
| Top 2.5% fitness | 80% lower death risk vs lowest quartile |
| Each 1 MET improvement | ~3.3% mortality risk reduction |
| 1 mL/kg/min VO2max increase | 3.3% mortality risk reduction |

**VO2max is the strongest predictor of survival.**

## Athlete's Heart: Physiological Remodeling

### Endurance (Aerobic) Training
- ↑ Left ventricular cavity size (eccentric hypertrophy)
- ↑ Wall thickness (proportional)
- ↑ Diastolic filling
- ↑ Stroke volume
- ↑ Cardiac output

### Resistance Training
- Concentric hypertrophy (wall thickness > cavity)
- ↑ Systolic function

### Physiological vs Pathological

| Feature | Athletic "Athlete's Heart" | Pathological |
|---------|---------------------------|-------------|
| Remodeling | Symmetric | Often asymmetric |
| Diastolic function | Normal or enhanced | Often impaired |
| Fibrosis | None | Present |
| Systolic function | Normal | May be impaired |
| Exercise capacity | ↑↑ above age norm | Below normal |

## Cardiac Rehabilitation

Exercise-based cardiac rehabilitation significantly reduces:
- Mortality in coronary heart disease patients
- Hospital admissions
- All-cause death risk

## Key Mechanism

Exercise-induced cardiac remodeling:
- Mechanical stress → signaling pathways
- IGF-1/GH → cardiac growth
- ↑ Fatty acid oxidation (maintained in physiological; reverted in pathological)
- Capillary density ∝ muscle mass
- ECM remodeling **without fibrosis** (key differentiator)
