# GI Health & Exercise (运动与胃肠道健康)

## Overview

Moderate exercise enhances gut microbiome diversity, SCFA production, gut barrier function, and GI tolerance. However, high-intensity exercise (>70% VO2max) can impair GI function. The gut "trains" like other systems with systematic conditioning.

## Exercise and Gut Microbiome

| Finding | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Exercise increases alpha diversity | +15-30% in most intervention studies |
| Enriched beneficial bacteria | *Faecalibacterium prausnitzii*, *Roseburia hominis*, *Akkermansia muciniphila* |
| Athletes vs sedentary | Greater *Christensenellaceae*, *Ruminococcaceae* |
| Single bout | Acute shifts; sustained change requires consistent training |
| Exercise + fiber | Synergistic effect on diversity |

**Rugby player study (Clarke 2017, Gut)**: Athletes had significantly greater microbial diversity vs age-matched sedentary controls.

## GI Adaptation (Gut Training)

The gut undergoes training adaptations similar to muscles:

| Training Adaptation | Effect |
|-------------------|--------|
| GI tolerance | Improved with systematic training |
| Gastric emptying at >70% VO2max | Can decrease by 50% — **trainable** |
| Exercise at 40-60% VO2max | Produces barrier benefits |
| Progressive training | Reduces GI distress during competition |

## GI Distress in Endurance Athletes

**Exercise-Induced GI Symptoms (EIGS)**: 40-70% of endurance athletes experience GI distress.

| Mechanism | Detail |
|----------|--------|
| Reduced intestinal blood flow | Splanchnic hypoperfusion during exercise |
| Mechanical vibration | Jostling of intestines |
| Thermoregulatory strain | Exacerbates barrier disruption |

**Prevention strategies**:
- Reduce high-fiber foods 36h pre-competition
- Limit fiber to 10-15g/day before race
- Low-FODMAP ingredients pre-race
- Systematic GI training

## Microbiome Mechanisms

| Pathway | Effect |
|---------|--------|
| Bile acid metabolism | Exercise → altered BA composition → microbiome shift |
| Gut motility | ↑ transit time → changed microbial habitat |
| IgA secretion | Exercise → ↑ mucosal immunity → shapes microbiome |
| Blood flow | Adaptive changes → altered microbial environment |
| Stress reduction | ↓ cortisol → improved diversity |
