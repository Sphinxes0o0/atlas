# Exercise Epigenetics (运动表观遗传学)

## Overview

Exercise induces profound adaptations through **epigenetic mechanisms** — changes in gene expression without altering DNA sequence. Key pathways include DNA methylation, telomere length, and histone modifications.

## DNA Methylation

| Effect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| Site-specific methylation changes | Correlates with improved metabolic function |
| Muscle oxidative phenotype | Exercise affects ACE promoter methylation |
| "Memory" effects | Retraining after detraining shows accelerated adaptations |

**MethylationEPIC arrays** (850,000 CpG sites) show acute and chronic training adaptations in skeletal muscle.

## Telomere Length

| Finding | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Strength training | Telomeres ~238 base pairs longer vs non-exercisers |
| Equivalent biological age reduction | ~3.9 years |
| Each 10 min/week strength training | ~6.7 base pairs ↑ telomere length |
| Optimal dose | ~60+ minutes/week strength training |

**HIIT shows superior effects** vs aerobic endurance for telomere length.

## Epigenetic Mechanisms

| Mechanism | Effect |
|-----------|--------|
| DNA methylation | Gene silencing/activation at specific sites |
| Histone modifications | Chromatin remodeling → transcription access |
| Telomere length | Cellular aging marker; exercise preserves length |
| Telomerase activity | Tightly regulated; indirect protective mechanisms |

## Key Evidence

| Study | Finding |
|-------|---------|
| NHANES 4,814 participants (Biology 2024) | Strength training → longer telomeres |
| NHANES 6,200 participants (JESS 2025) | Physical activity → delayed phenotypic aging |
| Umbrella review (JMIR Aging 2025) | Exercise → small-to-moderate telomere effect (ES=0.28) |

## Mechanisms of Telomere Protection

| Pathway | Effect |
|---------|--------|
| Reduced oxidative stress | Less telomere damage |
| Reduced inflammation | Less telomere attrition |
| Enhanced DNA repair | Better maintenance |
| ↑ telomerase gene expression | Potential elongation |

## Clinical Significance

- Epigenetic modifications may serve as **biomarkers for exercise responsiveness**
- Methylation patterns can indicate **metabolic health status**
- "Epigenetic memory" from training may accelerate readaptation
- Provides molecular basis for exercise → longevity connection
