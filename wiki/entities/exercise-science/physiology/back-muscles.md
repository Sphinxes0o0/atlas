# Back Muscles (背部肌群功能)

## Overview

The back musculature comprises multiple layers of muscles extending from the skull to the pelvis, organized into superficial, intermediate, and deep layers. These muscles control spinal position, enable trunk movement, and maintain posture against gravity.

## Scapular Kinematics

### Upper Trapezius
- Dominant in shrugging; often overactive in individuals with neck/shoulder pain
- Lower trap deficiency → upper trap dominance → rounded shoulders

### Serratus Anterior
- "Boxing muscle" — scapular protraction during push-up and punching
- Weakness → winging of scapula; proximal instability

### Rhomboids
- Major and minor; deep to trapezius
- Retract scapula and rotate downward to counter upper trap elevation

## Erector Spinae Group

Three columns (iliocostalis, longissimus, spinalis) spanning entire vertebral column:
- **Iliocostalis**: Laterally placed; neck and back extension
- **Longissimus**: Middle column; thoracic and cervical extension
- **Spinalis**: Medial column; smallest and most deep

Function in hip-hinge mechanics: erector spinae controls forward trunk lean during deadlift and kettlebell swings.

## Training Considerations

### Pulling Patterns
- **Horizontal rows**: Middle trapezius and rhomboid emphasis
- **Vertical pulls**: Lat emphasis with scapular depression
- **Face pulls**: Upper/middle trap and rear delt integration

### Scapular Stability
- **Y-T-W exercises**: Lower and middle trap activation
- **Wall slides**: Serratus anterior integration
- **Push-up plus**: Serratus anterior strengthening

### Posterior Chain
- **Good mornings**: Erector spinae emphasis with hip hinge
- **Reverse hyperextension**: Lumbar extension without spinal load
- **Prone Y-T-W**: Trapezius differentiation by angle

## Common Injuries

- **Upper trap trigger points**: Common in desk workers; referral to temple/migraine
- **Latissimus strain**: Common in gymnastics and tennis; shoulder pain
- **Erector spinae spasm**: Acute low back pain episodes
- **Serratus inhibition**: Scapular winging during push-ups
