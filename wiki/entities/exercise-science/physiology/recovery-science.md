# Recovery Science (恢复科学)

## Overview

Recovery science encompasses the biological and physiological processes that restore athletic performance following training or competition. Optimal recovery enables repeated high-quality training sessions and prevents overtraining syndrome.

## Recovery Mechanisms

### Neuromuscular Recovery
- **Central nervous system**: Motor cortex activation restoration; dopamine/serotonin rebalancing
- **Peripheral nervous system**: Neuromuscular junction restoration; acetylcholine reuptake
- **HRV monitoring**: Sympathetic-vagal balance as recovery biomarker

### Muscle Recovery
- **Protein synthesis**: MPS peaks 24-48h post-exercise; requires adequate amino acid availability
- **Satellite cell activation**: Muscle stem cells proliferate and fuse to repair damaged fibers
- **Mitochondrial biogenesis**: PGC-1α activation; restoration of oxidative capacity

### Metabolic Recovery
- **Glycogen resynthesis**: 4-6h post-exercise; carb/protein co-ingestion accelerates
- **Creatine phosphate restoration**: ATP-PCr system恢复需要 3-5 minutes of rest
- **Electrolyte balance**: Sodium-potassium pump restoration

## Evidence-Based Recovery Strategies

### Cold Water Immersion (CWI)
- **Mechanism**: Vasoconstriction reduces inflammation; neural浮躁
- **Effect**: DOMS reduction (perceived soreness ↓); potential protein synthesis inhibition
- **Protocol**: 10-15°C for 10-20 minutes; post-exercise timing

### Heat Therapy
- **Mechanism**: Vasodilation; HSP (heat shock protein) upregulation
- **Effect**: Improved flexibility; potential muscle adaptation enhancement
- **Application**: Chronic injury management; pre-rehabilitation

### Contrast Water Therapy (CWT)
- Alternating hot/cold immersion; combines vasodilation and vasoconstriction
- **Evidence**: Modest benefits for repeated sprint performance

### Compression Garments
- External pressure may enhance venous return; perception of reduced soreness
- **Evidence**: Small improvements in perceived recovery; minimal performance effect

### Sleep Optimization
- **Duration**: 7-9h/night for athletes; growth hormone peaks in deep sleep
- **Quality**: Sleep efficiency >85%; REM restoration
- **Napping**: 20-30min post-training naps; avoid >90min that disrupts night sleep

## Nutritional Recovery

### Protein Timing
- 25-40g protein every 3-4h; leucine threshold ~2.5g
- Pre-sleep casein (~30g) supports overnight MPS

### Carbohydrate
- 1.2-1.8g/kg/h for first 4h post-exercise
- Simple + complex carb combination optimal

### Hydration
- Replace fluid losses: 150% of weight lost during exercise
- Monitor urine color: pale yellow indicates adequate hydration

## Monitoring Recovery

### Biomarkers
- **HRV**: Morning HRV depression indicates incomplete recovery
- **RPE**: Session RPE increase suggests accumulated fatigue
- **CMJ**: Countermovement jump height as neuromuscular function test
- **Urine specific gravity**: Hydration status

### ACWR (Acute:Chronic Workload Ratio)
- Optimal range: 1.0-1.3
- >1.5: Increased injury risk
- <0.8: Detraining effect
