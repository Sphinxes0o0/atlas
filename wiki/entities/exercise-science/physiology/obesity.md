# Obesity & Exercise (肥胖与运动)

## Overview

Obesity is a chronic metabolic disease characterized by excess adiposity, systemic inflammation, and metabolic inflexibility. Exercise is a primary non-pharmacological intervention for obesity management, independent of weight loss.

## Body Composition

### Adipose Tissue Physiology
- **White adipose tissue (WAT)**: Energy storage; releases free fatty acids and adipokines
- **Brown adipose tissue (BAT)**: Non-shivering thermogenesis; dissipates energy as heat
- **Beige/brite adipose tissue**: WAT that acquires BAT-like properties with exercise

### Ectopic Fat Deposition
- **Intramuscular fat**: Impairs insulin signaling; affects muscle quality
- **Visceral fat**: Abdominal depot; inflammatory cytokine source
- **Hepatic fat**: NAFLD precursor; metabolic syndrome marker

## Exercise and Fat Loss

### Energy Expenditure
- **Acute**: Exercise EE variable based on intensity/duration
- **Chronic**: EPOC (excess post-exercise oxygen consumption); 24-48h elevated metabolism
- **Total daily energy expenditure**: Exercise alone insufficient; requires diet + activity

### Exercise Modality Effects

| Modality | Fat Oxidation | Metabolic Adaptation |
|----------|--------------|-------------------|
| MICT | Higher absolute fat burn | Improved fatty acid transport |
| HIIT | Lower absolute but higher per time | Improved insulin sensitivity |
| Resistance | Minimal during exercise | Increased muscle mass → basal metabolism ↑ |

### Fat Oxidation
- Exercise intensity at 45-65% VO2max maximizes fat oxidation
- Training status improves fat oxidation efficiency
- Carnitine supplementation may enhance fatty acid transport

## Exercise for Metabolic Health

### Insulin Sensitivity
- Single bout improves insulin-stimulated glucose uptake 24-72h
- AMPK activation increases GLUT4 translocation
- Muscle contraction bypasses insulin signaling defects in T2DM

### Inflammation
- Exercise reduces CRP, IL-6, TNF-α
- IL-6 from muscle (myokine) has anti-inflammatory effects
- Regular exercise prevents chronic low-grade inflammation

### VO2max
- Strongest predictor of all-cause mortality independent of BMI
- Exercise training improves VO2max 15-25% in previously sedentary

## Clinical Considerations

### Exercise Prescription
- **Frequency**: 5-7 days/week for weight management; 3-5 days for health
- **Duration**: 30-60 min moderate intensity; can be accumulated in 10min bouts
- **Intensity**: Moderate (40-60% HRR) for fat oxidation; vigorous for EPOC
- **Mode**: Combination aerobic + resistance for body composition

### Barriers in Obesity
- Joint pain limits weight-bearing exercise
- Start low-intensity; aquatic exercise reduces joint stress
- Behavioral strategies for adherence

### Weight Loss Plateaus
- Metabolic adaptation: BMR decreases ~15% with significant weight loss
- Training adaptations: Improved efficiency decreases EE at same workload
- Strategy: Increase intensity, add resistance training, dietary re-evaluation
