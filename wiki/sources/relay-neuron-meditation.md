# Source: Meditation — Mindfulness for Sport Performance

## Summary

正念冥想训练在运动领域的应用研究综合，涵盖竞赛焦虑管理、注意力改善、恢复增强，以及潜在风险（解离反应、回避应对、正念不能替代体能训练的科学证据）。

## Key Topics

**Benefits:**
- Competition anxiety reduction (g = -0.67)
- Attention and focus improvement (d = 0.68-0.82)
- Recovery enhancement (HRV improvement)
- Mental resilience for athletes
- Neural plasticity evidence (prefrontal, amygdala)
- 26% adverse events; 3.4% severe

**Risks:**
- Dissociative reactions (8.7%)
- Pseudo-acceptance avoidance coping (14%)
- Time cost vs training adaptation trade-off
- Cannot substitute physical training (neurobiological evidence)
