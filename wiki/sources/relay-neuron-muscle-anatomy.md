## Key Findings

**Shoulder:** Rotator cuff force couples maintain glenohumeral centering; supraspinatus initiates abduction; infraspinatus provides 60-70% of external rotation torque.

**Upper Arm:** Biceps generates 40% of supination torque; triceps long head also extends shoulder; brachialis is strongest pure elbow flexor.

**Chest/Back:** Trapezius force couples (upper+middle+lower) essential for scapular upward rotation; serratus anterior-lower trapezius force couple controls scapulothoracic rhythm.

**Core:** Transversus abdominis activates 20-50ms before limb movement (feed-forward); multifidus atrophies rapidly with back pain; McGill big three are evidence-based.

**Hip:** Gluteus maximus is largest muscle by cross-sectional area; gluteus medius prevents Trendelenburg drop; piriformis syndrome is sciatic nerve entrapment (not true radiculopathy).

**Thigh:** VMO angle (55°) critical for patellar tracking; hamstrings strain is most common sports injury; Nordic curl eccentric training reduces strain recurrence.

**Lower Leg:** Soleus more active than gastrocnemius during running at moderate speeds; Achilles stores ~35% elastic energy; tibialis anterior eccentrically controls plantarflexion during stance.
