# Source: Psychology — Exercise Addiction & Overtraining Risks

## Summary

运动心理学风险文献综合，涵盖运动成瘾（9.5%患病率）、过度训练综合征（OTS）、相对能量缺乏综合征（RED-S）、运动依赖的神经生物学机制。

## Key Topics

- Exercise addiction prevalence (9.5% at-risk)
- Overtraining syndrome (20-40% athletes affected)
- RED-S psychological consequences
- Neuroimaging evidence (reward circuits, DMN)
- Dopaminergic reward system alterations
- RPE misperception mechanisms
- Prevention and intervention strategies
