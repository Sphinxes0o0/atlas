## Scope

Thermal therapies for athletic recovery: cold water immersion (CWI), heat therapy (sauna/hot water), contrast therapy (hot-cold alternation), whole body cryotherapy (WBC).

## Key Findings

1. **CWI** reduces DOMS but attenuates resistance training adaptations (anabolic signaling blunted, less muscle growth vs passive recovery after 7 weeks)
2. **Heat therapy** (42°C hot water) superior to cold/warm for muscle repair markers; HSP70 activation promotes protein folding and remodeling
3. **Contrast therapy** (12°C/42°C alternation) best for fatigue elimination and lactate clearance
4. **WBC** reduces IL-1β ~40%, increases IL-10; does NOT blunt hypertrophy adaptations (unlike CWI)
5. Thermal choice should match training goals — cold for soreness, heat for tissue repair
