## Scope

Altitude/hypoxia training for endurance athletes: HIF pathway, EPO, training methods (HiLo, HiHiLo, LHTL, LoHi), performance benefits, AMS/HAPE risks.

## Key Findings

### Mechanisms
- HIF-1α stabilization → EPO (3-5 days), Hb mass (2-3 weeks), capillary density (VEGF)
- Optimal altitude: 2500-3000m (FiO₂ 14.5-15.5%)

### Training Methods
| Method | Description | Evidence |
|--------|-------------|----------|
| HiLo | Live high-train low | ★★★ |
| HiHiLo | Live high-train high-low | ★★★ |
| LHTL | Live high-train low (hypoxic sleep only) | ★★ |
| LoHi | Live low-train high (altitude camp) | ★★ |

### Performance Benefits
- VO₂max ↑3-7%, 5000m ↓1-3%, lactate threshold ↑5-10%
- 22-day camp at 2320m: 800m freestyle ↑1.2-2.8%

### Risks
- AMS: 30-50% above 2500m; acetazolamide 125-250mg bid reduces 50-75%
- HAPE: 0.6-6% at 2500-4000m; nifedipine treatment
- Excessive erythrocytosis: Hct>50-55% increases thrombosis risk
- Sleep disruption, URTI risk ↑OR 1.5-1.8
