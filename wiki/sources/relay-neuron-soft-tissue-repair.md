## Scope

Soft tissue injury repair and healing: tendinopathy rehabilitation, muscle strain mechanisms, fascia pain syndromes, ligament healing, PRP therapy, ACWR injury prediction, age-related degeneration.

## Key Findings

### Repair
1. **Eccentric loading** is most evidence-based tendon treatment (15.2-point VISA-A improvement for Achilles tendinopathy)
2. **PRP** moderately effective for chronic wounds (RR=1.47 closure) and knee OA; mixed for tendinopathy
3. **Tendon neuroplastic training** addresses central sensitization in tendinopathy
4. **Stem cells** (UC-MSCs) show promise for tendon regeneration

### Risks
1. **Previous injury** is strongest predictor — hamstring recurrence 16-60%; Achilles OR=2.56
2. **ACWR >1.5** increases injury risk 2-3x; optimal range 1.0-1.25
3. **Age** increases risk via AGEs accumulation, collagen cross-linking, reduced satellite cells
4. **Myofascial trigger points** affect 30-85% of pain patients; treated with ischemic compression
