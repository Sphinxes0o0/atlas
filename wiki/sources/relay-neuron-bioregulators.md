## Scope

Recovery technologies: whole body cryotherapy (WBC), photobiomodulation (PBM/red light), intermittent hypoxic training (IHT), breathing techniques (pranayama, Wim Hof, IMT), electromyostimulation (EMS).

## Key Findings

### WBC
- Reduces IL-1β ~40%, increases IL-10; ~80% studies show DOMS reduction
- Does NOT blunt resistance training adaptations (unlike CWI)
- Risks: cardiovascular stress, bronchospasm, frostbite; contraindicated in hypertension/arrhythmia

### Photobiomodulation
- Reduces DOMS soreness 31% at 48h; increases mitochondrial complex I activity 27%
- Optimal: 800-850nm, 20-60 J/muscle group
- Risks: biphasic dose response, device quality variability, limited penetration depth

### IHT (Intermittent Hypoxic Training)
- Improves 3000m by 2.8%; Hb mass +4.8% in responders
- ~40% non-responders (primarily low iron stores)
- Risks: AMS (25-70% above 2500m), HAPE, sleep disruption

### Breathing Techniques
- Pranayama: 5km time ↓3.2%, VO2max ↑7.8%
- Wim Hof: IL-6 response ↓47% to endotoxin
- IMT: time to exhaustion SMD=0.58
- Risks: hyperventilation induces cerebral hypoperfusion, respiratory alkalosis

### EMS
- High-frequency: DOMS pain ↓38% at 48h; lactate clearance ↑41%
- Combined with training: CSA ↑18% vs 12% with training alone
- Risks: rhabdomyolysis at >100mA, nerve damage, burns
